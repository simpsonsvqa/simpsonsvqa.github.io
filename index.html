What it is

SimpsonsVQA is a unified VQA resource for answering questions about cartoon images, spotting irrelevant questions, and judging whether a proposed answer is correct, incorrect, or ambiguous — aimed at early education and assistive inquiry systems.

Why cartoons

Most VQA benchmarks lean on photorealistic photos. SimpsonsVQA probes the cartoon domain gap and asks whether today’s vision–language models can still reason when the world looks drawn, bright, and culturally familiar.
